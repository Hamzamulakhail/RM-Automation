User Data Deletion Instructions

RM Automation stores your workspace license identifier, connected Google email and encrypted refresh token, Facebook account/Page identifiers and encrypted access tokens, schedule configuration, and operational logs inside Cloudflare D1.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Sign in to your RM Automation workspace with your License Key (or as Super Admin).
  2. Disconnect Google Drive: Go to the Accounts & Drive tab and click Disconnect on the Google Drive card. This immediately revokes the OAuth token with Google and deletes your Google email and encrypted refresh token from D1. You can also revoke access from your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services.
  3. Delete Facebook Accounts & Pages: On the Accounts & Drive tab, click Delete next to any connected Facebook account or Page entry. This immediately deletes the account record, encrypted tokens, linked Pages, and scheduled plan rows from D1.
  4. Full Workspace Removal: The Super Admin can delete a customer License Key under the Licenses tab to cascade-delete all associated accounts, Pages, schedules, blocked items, and logs immediately.

For manual removal requests, contact the operator of this RM Automation deployment.
