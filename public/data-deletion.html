User Data Deletion Instructions

RM Automation stores your Facebook account identifier, Page identifiers, encrypted access tokens, schedule configuration, and operational logs inside your private Cloudflare D1 database.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Open the RM Automation admin panel and sign in.
  2. Go to the Accounts tab and click Delete next to any connected Facebook account. This immediately deletes the account record, encrypted user token, linked Pages, encrypted Page tokens, and scheduled plan rows from D1.
  3. To revoke app permissions on Facebook directly: open Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), select RM Automation, and click Remove.
  4. To wipe all remaining logs or configuration, delete or reset the Cloudflare D1 database (page-poster-db) from your Cloudflare Dashboard.

For questions or manual removal requests, contact the operator of this RM Automation instance.
