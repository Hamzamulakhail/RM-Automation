Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

RM Automation provides authorized workspace owners and licensees with automated scheduling and publishing from their connected Google Drive folders to their managed Facebook Pages. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, how it is protected, and how you can remove your data at any time.

1. Information We Process

  • Authentication & Workspace Identifiers: Session cookies (HttpOnly) and workspace license identifiers required to keep you signed in securely.
  • Facebook / Meta Data: Access tokens you explicitly connect, your Facebook account/Page names, Page IDs, and Page profile picture URLs used solely to display your Pages and publish scheduled posts on your behalf via Meta Graph API.
  • Google Drive Data: Google OAuth tokens and the specific Drive folder IDs you choose inside the app so RM Automation can read scheduled media files and captions (captions.txt, caption.txt, or Google Docs) and move published items to Trash when configured.
  • Operational Logs & Queue Records: Scheduled slot timestamps, file identifiers, posting outcomes, and error diagnostics needed to run reliable automated publishing.

2. How We Use Information

Your connected tokens and content are used strictly to execute the automation actions you configure:

  • Listing your authorized Facebook Pages and Google Drive folders inside your private workspace.
  • Fetching queued media and matching captions prior to your scheduled time slots.
  • Publishing photos, videos, or Reels to your selected Facebook Pages at your chosen timezone and slots.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data, tokens, or media content with third parties or advertisers.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

RM Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access is strictly limited to reading user-selected Drive folders, processing scheduled media/captions, and performing authorized cleanup for those folders.

4. Data Security & 7-Day Automatic Retention

  • All Facebook and Google OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-GCM encryption before storage.
  • Operational event logs and completed post queue records older than 7 days are automatically deleted from the database.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

You retain full control over your connected accounts and stored data and may revoke access or delete data immediately at any time:

  • Disconnect Google Drive: Open Accounts & Drive inside RM Automation and click Disconnect. Stored Google refresh/access tokens are immediately erased. You can also revoke access from your Google Account Security permissions.
  • Remove Facebook Pages or Accounts: Open Accounts & Drive or Pages & Schedule and click Delete on any connected Facebook account or Page to permanently remove its encrypted tokens and schedules. You may also remove the app under Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.
  • Full Workspace Removal: Workspace administrators can delete an entire license key from the dashboard, which immediately cascades and permanently deletes all associated accounts, Pages, schedules, and logs.
