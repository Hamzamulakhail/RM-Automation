Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

RM Automation is a multi-workspace social publishing automation platform hosted on Cloudflare Workers that publishes scheduled photos, videos, and captions from a user's connected Google Drive folders to authorized Facebook Pages.

Information We Process

  • Google Account & Google Drive (OAuth 2.0): With your explicit consent via Google Sign-In (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email), we store your Google account email address and an AES-256-GCM encrypted OAuth refresh token in Cloudflare D1 solely to list folders you select, read caption files (captions.txt) and media files, update caption files after posting, and move posted media files to your Google Drive Trash.
  • Facebook Accounts & Pages: Facebook account/Page IDs, names, and access tokens pasted directly by you inside your workspace. All tokens are stored in Cloudflare D1 using AES-256-GCM encryption and are never returned in plaintext to the browser.
  • License & Workspace Data: Workspace license metadata, Page schedules, queue status, and diagnostic event logs (automatically pruned after 30 days).

How Data Is Used & Shared

Data is used strictly within your isolated workspace to publish content from your Google Drive folders to your selected Facebook Pages and report delivery status. RM Automation does not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data or Facebook data to third parties or use it for advertising, profiling, or model training; requests are sent only to Google Drive API and Meta Graph API as required to perform scheduled posting.

Security

All Google OAuth refresh tokens and Facebook access tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM before being persisted to Cloudflare D1. Access to each workspace is protected by an admin password or unique License Key and signed HttpOnly session cookies.

Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You may disconnect Google Drive or delete stored Facebook accounts, Pages, and encrypted tokens at any time directly inside your workspace, or follow the steps on the Data Deletion page.
